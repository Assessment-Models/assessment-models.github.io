GOVERNANCE & ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Built for Auditability, Provenance, and Normative Rigor

Enterprise capability assessments frequently suffer from opaque weighting, subjective evaluator bias, and proprietary lock-in. Our specifications treat maturity models as version-controlled, formally verifiable software artifacts.

Open Peer Moderation & RFC Lifecycle

All taxonomy revisions undergo public Request for Comments (RFC) review by enterprise architects and domain moderators prior to semantic version tagging.

Deterministic Calculation Over Heuristic Guesswork

Every composite score is derived from explicit mathematical aggregation functions with documented sensitivity bounds and zero black-box heuristics.

Full Provenance & Evidence Traceability

Assessment outputs bind directly to immutable schema versions and verifiable architectural artifacts, satisfying internal audit and regulatory scrutiny.

NORMATIVE GOVERNANCE DOCUMENT

Enterprise Assessment & Blueprint Governance Charter

Article I — Normative Determinism & Structural Integrity

All assessment models, maturity rubrics, and architectural blueprints published under assessment-models.github.io must satisfy Mutually Exclusive and Collectively Exhaustive (MECE) structural criteria. Subjective scoring heuristics without deterministic mathematical definitions are prohibited from core releases.

Article II — Open Moderation & Version Provenance

Schema modifications require peer review by at least two domain maintainers and automated CI verification via mece-ontology-validator. Every released artifact carries a cryptographic checksum and adheres strictly to Semantic Versioning (SemVer 2.0.0).

Article III — Permissive Enterprise Adoption

Software validation engines and scoring runtimes are licensed under Apache-2.0. Assessment taxonomies, documentation, and reference blueprints are licensed under CC-BY-4.0 to guarantee unrestricted enterprise adoption and audit attribution.
